# Design Prompts for BrainGym Visual Redesign

Use these prompts to generate premium illustration concepts for a modern brain enhancement app. The style should be sleek, futuristic, and adult-friendly with cool gradients, subtle depth, and a premium UI feel.

---

## 1. Futuristic AI Brain

Prompt:

"Create a premium mobile app illustration of a futuristic AI brain. The design should feature a translucent brain silhouette with glowing neural nodes, delicate circuit-like pathways, and ethereal gradient lighting in blue, purple, and cyan. Use clean shapes, subtle glassmorphism, and soft glowing depth to create a sense of intelligence and high-end technology. The style should be modern, minimal, and elegant, suitable for a top-tier cognitive training app onboarding screen."

Why it works:
- Emphasizes advanced AI and intelligence
- Builds trust with a science-inspired aesthetic
- Feels high-value and modern

---

## 2. Brain Gym / Mental Workout

Prompt:

"Create a premium mental workout illustration for a brain training app. Show a stylized brain formed from dynamic motion lines, activity rings, and energy arcs that evoke movement and effort. Include subtle progress markers, soft glowing accents, and a cool-tech gradient palette with indigo, violet, and teal. The look should be sophisticated and adult-oriented, conveying focused mental training without cartoonish gym imagery."

Why it works:
- Positions cognitive training as purposeful and energetic
- Makes mental fitness feel active and rewarding
- Appeals to users who want habit-building and progress

---

## 3. Neural Network Growth

Prompt:

"Generate a premium illustration of neural network growth for a brain enhancement app. Show branching neural pathways expanding outward from a central node, with glowing connection points, layered translucent planes, and subtle depth shadows. Use rich blues, cyans, and violet gradients to create a polished tech-science atmosphere. The design should look refined, elegant, and suitable for an app hero or feature section."

Why it works:
- Evokes learning, memory strengthening, and progress
- Appears credible and science-backed
- Ideal for users who want measurable cognitive improvement

---

## 4. Cognitive Transformation Journey

Prompt:

"Create a premium illustration of a cognitive transformation journey. Show a sleek, curved path or wave moving from soft muted tones into bright, energized gradients. Include refined milestone icons such as spark nodes, a trophy, and a brain pulse to represent growth. The style should be clean, modern, and inspirational, emphasizing a smooth transition from mental fog to clarity."

Why it works:
- Communicates progress and long-term improvement
- Inspires curiosity and personal growth
- Works well for onboarding and marketing narratives

## 5. Brain Power Achievement System

Prompt:

"Create a premium achievement system illustration for a brain app. Feature a polished brain icon combined with elegant badge and trophy elements, progress rings, and subtle star bursts. Use a sophisticated palette of deep navy, cyan, violet, and warm silver highlights. The style should feel rewarding, aspirational, and mature—perfect for a high-end cognitive training brand."

## Additional Recommendations

### Color palette
- Deep Indigo: `#1A2144`
- Electric Blue: `#4F7DFF`
- Radiant Violet: `#8B5CF6`
- Cyan Glow: `#22D3EE`
- Soft Teal: `#7DD3FC`
- Warm Silver: `#F8FAFC`
- Dark Charcoal: `#0F172A`
- Light Frost: `#F8FAFC`

### Typography guidance
- Primary: Inter, Sora, or Manrope
- Headline: Sora SemiBold, Lexend, or Space Grotesk
- Use strong hierarchy with clean letterforms and ample spacing

### Visual system notes
- Keep illustrations minimal and elegant, with premium gradient depth
- Avoid flat cartoon faces, generic icons, and pastel childlike motifs
- Use neural/AI metaphors, clean tech geometry, and subtle glowing light
- Ensure consistency across hero, onboarding, and marketing visuals

---

## Execution tips
- Use the strongest prompt language for premium, adult-oriented design.
- In image tools, prefer terms like "sleek," "minimal," "futuristic," "premium," "glowing depth," and "technology-inspired." 
- Add "for mobile app onboarding screen" when generating hero and onboarding art.
- Keep compositions clean and avoid clutter.
