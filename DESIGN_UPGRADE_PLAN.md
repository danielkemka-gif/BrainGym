# BrainGym Visual Identity Upgrade Plan

This document outlines a premium visual redesign for BrainGym focused on user enjoyment, trust, and conversion.

## Goals
- Create a modern, professional brain training brand.
- Appeal to adults aged 18-60.
- Communicate intelligence, memory improvement, focus, and growth.
- Avoid childish or generic clip-art styling.
- Make the app feel premium, futuristic, and trustworthy.

## 5 Illustration Concepts

### 1. Futuristic AI Brain
- Central translucent brain silhouette.
- Glowing neural nodes and data lines.
- Soft blue/purple gradients with cyan highlights.
- AI-inspired interface overlays and subtle motion.
- Why it works: positions the app as advanced and science-backed.

### 2. Brain Gym / Mental Workout
- Abstract brain composed of kinetic curves and activity rings.
- Progress markers, pulse arcs, and motion streaks.
- A sense of active, intentional training.
- Why it works: makes cognitive practice feel purposeful and rewarding.

### 3. Neural Network Growth
- Branching, radiant neural pathways growing outward.
- Connected nodes with subtle glow and depth.
- Transparent layered planes for modern depth.
- Why it works: evokes learning, strengthening, and clarity.

### 4. Cognitive Transformation Journey
- A path or curve moving from calm to luminous.
- Milestones represented by icons like spark, trophy, and shield.
- A polished transition from foggy to sharp.
- Why it works: resonates with users seeking long-term improvement.

### 5. Brain Power Achievement System
- Brain icon combined with premium badge/trophy shapes.
- Progress rings, small stars, and elegant reward elements.
- Use of refined gold/cyan highlights on a dark gradient.
- Why it works: makes progress feel tangible and motivating.

- Cyan Glow: `#22D3EE`
- Soft Teal: `#7DD3FC`
- Warm Silver: `#F8FAFC`
- Dark Charcoal: `#0F172A`
- Light Frost: `#F8FAFC`

## Typography

- Primary font: Inter, Sora, or Manrope.
- Secondary headline: Sora SemiBold, Lexend, or Space Grotesk.
- Use strong hierarchy with bold headings and calm body text.
- Maintain generous line-height and comfortable spacing.

## Icon System

- Replace emoji-style icons with a custom unified icon set.
- Use modern outline + filled styles.
- Keep icons rounded, minimal, and consistent.
- Recommended icon motifs:
  - neural node
  - brain pulse
  - rocket/growth
  - badge/trophy
  - insight spark
  - shield/trust
  - progress circle
